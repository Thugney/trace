## Content quantity versus educational completeness

- Verified Udir curriculum codes, official goal checkpoint URLs and grading-stage logic: **Ready for code**.
- Short original lesson drafts: **250** (5 per grade × subject; typically 52–91 article words).
- Practice items: **500** (1 keyed MCQ + 1 open reflection per lesson).
- Flashcards: **750** (3 per lesson).
- Draft review papers: **50** with 500 items, including reused practice questions.
- Comprehensive, individually crosswalked LK20 curricula and educator-approved full lessons: **NOT COMPLETE**.

**Do not deploy or promote the research files as a finished curriculum.** New lessons and reviews are drafts, and the live app must keep access off until human review. Agent responsibility is implementation/testing using these inputs—not browsing Udir, writing new curricular material, or inventing missing lesson content.
