## 2. Verified goal themes (paraphrased)

These goal aliases are **local FamilyHub references, not invented official Udir identifiers**. Each theme is a short paraphrase of content verified on the linked subject goal page. The linked official page takes precedence if an editorial description is imprecise.

### Matematikk — `MAT01-06` (after Grade 8)

Udir source: https://www.udir.no/lk20/mat01-06/kompetansemaal-og-vurdering/kv1027?lang=nob

- **`M8-FACTOR`**: Utforske faktorisering og primtallsfaktorisering og anvende dette i brøkregning.
- **`M8-POW`**: Regne med potenser og kvadratrøtter og forklare metoder og svar.
- **`M8-PATTERN`**: Beskrive og generalisere tallmønstre og geometriske mønstre med ord og algebra.
- **`M8-EXPR`**: Lage algebraiske uttrykk med variabler, konstanter og tall fra praktiske situasjoner.
- **`M8-EQU`**: Lage og løse likninger i praktiske situasjoner og kontrollere løsningene.
- **`M8-INEQ`**: Bruke strategier for ulikheter og begrunne hvilke løsninger som er gyldige.
- **`M8-FUNC`**: Utforske praktiske funksjonssammenhenger og vise dem som tabeller, grafer og uttrykk.

### Norsk — `NOR01-08` (after Grade 10)

Udir source: https://www.udir.no/lk20/nor01-08/kompetansemaal-og-vurdering/kv1110?lang=nob

- **`NO10-ARG`**: Argumentere saklig i muntlige og skriftlige sjangre, med fagbegreper og retoriske virkemidler.
- **`NO10-READ`**: Lese og reflektere over skjønnlitteratur og sakprosa, sjangertrekk og språklige virkemidler.
- **`NO10-SOURCE`**: Vurdere kilder kritisk, markere sitater og vise til kilder på en etterrettelig måte.
- **`NO10-WRITE`**: Utforme og bearbeide tekster med tekstbinding, tegnsetting og rettskriving for ulike formål.
- **`NO10-LANG`**: Utforske språklig variasjon og mangfold og forklare bakgrunnen for bokmål og nynorsk.

### Engelsk — `ENG01-06` (after Grade 10)

Udir source: https://www.udir.no/lk20/eng01-06/kompetansemaal-og-vurdering/kv1033?lang=eng

- **`EN10-WRITE`**: Write and revise formal and informal texts adapted to audience, purpose and context.
- **`EN10-READ`**: Read, discuss, interpret and reflect on different texts, including young-adult fiction.
- **`EN10-GRAM`**: Apply word classes, sentence structure, spelling and text coherence in communication.
- **`EN10-SPEAK`**: Use varied vocabulary and follow-up questions in coherent conversations.
- **`EN10-SOURCE`**: Read nonfiction critically, evaluate source reliability and cite information responsibly.

### Naturfag — `NAT01-05` (after Grade 10)

Udir source: https://www.udir.no/lk20/nat01-05/kompetansemaal-og-vurdering/kv1078?lang=nob

- **`NA10-CELL`**: Sammenligne celler fra ulike organismer og forklare sammenheng mellom struktur og funksjon.
- **`NA10-INVEST`**: Formulere hypoteser, undersøke variabler, samle og vurdere data og følge sikkerhetstiltak.
- **`NA10-MATTER`**: Bruke atommodeller, grunnstoffer og periodesystemet; undersøke reaksjoner og massebevaring.
- **`NA10-ENERGY`**: Beskrive energibevaring og energikvalitet og undersøke hvordan energi omdannes og flyttes.
- **`NA10-ECO`**: Utforske samspill i økosystemer og kretsløp, herunder fotosyntese og celleånding.

### Samfunnsfag — `SAF01-05` (after Grade 10)

Udir source: https://www.udir.no/lk20/saf01-05/kompetansemaal-og-vurdering/kv1151?lang=nob

- **`SA10-DEMO`**: Beskrive det politiske systemet og velferdssamfunnet i Norge og reflektere over makt.
- **`SA10-SOURCE`**: Vurdere kilder, algoritmisk påvirkning, digital offentlighet og personvern.
- **`SA10-GEO`**: Sammenligne geografiske, historiske og politiske forhold som påvirker bosetting og levekår.
- **`SA10-HISTORY`**: Undersøke samfunnsendring og forklare årsaker og følger av utvalgte historiske konflikter.
- **`SA10-IDENTITY`**: Reflektere over identitet, kultur, mangfold og menneske- og urfolksrettigheter.

## 4. Content quality contract for Codex

For **each of 25 topics**, author:
- An original age-appropriate lesson/article with sections and examples (target 500–800 words where appropriate; split lengthy texts).
- About 10 **distinct, answer-validated** practice questions, mixing knowledge, application and reasoning, with explanations.
- About 10 editable flashcards that reuse FamilyHub's personal-card/review functionality.
- An exit ticket with observable evidence of understanding and a proposed mapping to local goal aliases.
- A visible source/review record: Udir code, official goal stage (8 or 10), URL, content version, reviewer, review date.

**Subject checks:** In mathematics, solve and recalculate all numeric keys and accept equivalent correct answers; in Norwegian and English, include real reading/writing and do not reduce essays to MCQs; in Science, distinguish models from observations and use safe investigations; in Social Studies, preserve civic neutrality, diversity and privacy, and support factual claims with reliable evidence.

**Editorial controls:** Udir curricula are reference material, not freely copyable textbook articles. Do not bulk-copy source pages, local school plans or third-party textbook prose. Keep generated material in **draft** until a human has reviewed factual accuracy, pedagogical appropriateness, competence-goal mapping, answer keys and language. An AI-generated lesson is not automatically "Udir-verified".

## 5. Assessment constraints from Udir

- From **Grade 8**, Norwegian school subjects use grades **1–6** under the current regulations.
- **No official Udir percentage-to-grade conversion** is supplied by these sources. The PR #9 fixed percentage bands are an internal exercise heuristic, NOT an Udir grading standard.
- Ordinary practice should primarily give qualitative explanations and next steps.
- Mock exams may show clearly **non-official**, rubric-backed indicative results, but should return `insufficient_evidence` for insufficient coverage or open-ended answers that require human review. Never suggest that a small test predicts standpunkt.
- Udir states standpunkt reflects holistic competence, not a single exam or a numerical average of tests.

## 6. Implementation handoff

1. Read `docs/familyhub-learn-architecture.md`, `docs/familyhub-learn-implementation.md` and the accompanying JSON topic map before editing code.
2. Work from the **approved integration baseline** when available; otherwise use `feature/familyhub-learn` only for content development. Do not merge PR #8/#9 or deploy automatically.
3. Correct five outdated Udir codes and the mapped goal references in `backend/src/services/learn/curriculum.ts`. Preserve the five existing topic slugs, reviewed lesson history, attempt IDs and assignments. If a curriculum goal changes, use a **new revision and safe mapping migration**, not an in-place overwrite of approved content.
4. Add 20 topics by extending existing Learn fixtures and existing activity/flashcard engines; no new LMS/quiz engine. Seed idempotently; lesson revisions remain immutable and new lessons are `draft`.
5. Add tests for 25-topic catalog shape, stable identifiers, referential goal mappings, reviewed/draft visibility, seed reruns, answer validity and cross-family authorization. Validate a reviewable lesson per subject first, then expand topic coverage.
6. Submit an **implementation PR** with all test evidence; do not turn on the parent pilot or publish AI-generated text without human review.

**Out-of-scope:** This research document and JSON do **not** contain 25 finished articles, quiz banks or school-approved mock exam papers. They replace the initial online curriculum discovery step; Codex authors implementation/content, and a human publishes approved work.
