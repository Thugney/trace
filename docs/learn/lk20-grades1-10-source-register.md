### Grade-to-checkpoint rule

- Matematikk: 1. trinn maps forward to **after 2. trinn**; 2.–10. trinn each have their own official checkpoint.
- Norsk, engelsk, naturfag and samfunnsfag: grades 1–2 → checkpoint **2**; grades 3–4 → **4**; grades 5–7 → **7**; grades 8–10 → **10**.
- The grade-by-grade topic plans are **local teaching sequences** toward those checkpoints. Never label a 5th-grade Norsk topic as an official Udir "after Grade 5" competence goal.
- Early English needs supportive Norwegian explanation and age-appropriate English output; older students should primarily encounter English reading/writing.

### Assessment

- Grades **1–7**: feedback on understanding, practice and progress **without numeric school grades**.
- Grades **8–10**: rubric-backed mock exams may show clearly labelled **non-official** indicative 1–6 with evidence limitations. No official Udir percentage cutoffs exist for this product.
- Source: [Opplæringsforskrifta § 9-3](https://www.udir.no/regelverkstolkninger/opplaring/forskrift-om-grunnskoleopplaringa-og-den-vidaregaande-opplaringa-opplaringsforskrifta/tredje-delen--fellesreglar-for-grunnskoleopplaringa-og-den-vidaregaande-opplaringa-for-barn-og-unge/kapittel-9-individuell-vurdering/i.-generelle-reglar/-9-3-karakterar-i-fag/) and relevant Udir assessment guidance.

### Content implementation contract

For each subject/grade, supply and implement:
1. Stable original content ID, grade, subject, topic, order and local strand.
2. Direct Udir stage/source URL and official stage, no fake official goal code.
3. Original short reading lesson with a specific worked example and misconception correction, not merely a topic title.
4. Explicitly keyed practice, original flashcards, and an explanation/reflection task.
5. Source/review status and version; draft by default, human review before publishing.
6. An instructional-language profile and an assessment policy appropriate to the grade.

**Critical:** This source register establishes *where and at what checkpoint* official curricula apply. A generated lesson library, however broad, must not be described as exhaustive official goal coverage until every official competence goal is individually crosswalked and audited by an educator.

**No live-app modifications**: Data-only research. Import with new additive versions; never overwrite existing reviewed lessons or change production assignments.
